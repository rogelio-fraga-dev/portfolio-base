About me

I am a full stack developer and I work from the domain model to the deploy. At InovaLayer, an innovation project linked to UFU and funded by FAPEMIG, I am the lead engineer: I own the Spring Boot API, the web portal and the desktop app in Angular and Electron, and the AWS infrastructure, versioned in Terraform and shipped by CI/CD.

I also teach Web Programming Techniques at a technical school, on a platform I built and operate myself. Having 73 students using the system during class raises the bar: migrations rehearsed against real data, deploys locked out of class hours, and the bare minimum of personal data, because they are minors.

How I work

Infrastructure as code

InovaLayer's AWS (network, database, CDN, e-mail, alarms and cost ceiling) is provisioned in Terraform and reviewed in the plan before every apply.

Tests that prove the rule

InovaLayer's cost engine is validated against UFU's official spreadsheet: 24 of 24 line items, maximum error of 0.0024%.

Secure by default

At Razor, an audit closed three critical flaws before the pilot. The webhook is fail-closed and login attempts are rate limited.

Real database in tests

Integration with Testcontainers against real PostgreSQL and Redis in CI, not mocks. That is more than 1,300 tests across my projects.

Deploys without surprises

OIDC instead of static AWS keys, deploys through SSM with port 22 closed, and pg_dump before every migration.

Decisions written down

ADRs, C4 model and a context log in every project: the reason behind each choice is documented, not kept in someone's head.

Multi-tenant SaaS · AI

Razor: the barbershop operating system

The evolution of BarberBot: from a WhatsApp bot into a SaaS where each barbershop has an AI that answers, books and charges over WhatsApp, plus a web panel with schedule, cash register, commissions, inventory and a subscription club.

  • Legacy refactoring: a ~1,000-line central class became cohesive services, the empty catch blocks are gone, and conversation state moved from 12 in-memory maps to a StateStore port backed by Redis.
  • Fail-closed tenant isolation: a security audit closed three critical flaws before the pilot, among them a forgeable webhook and the pairing QR code exposed without login.
  • 486 tests, with integration against real PostgreSQL and Redis through Testcontainers in CI, and 34 Flyway migrations.

ObraCerta: a construction marketplace

Connects construction professionals to the people hiring them, with scheduling, double-blind reviews, sealed bids on jobs and plan-based subscriptions. An end-to-end MVP, installable as a PWA.
